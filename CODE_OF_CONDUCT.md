# Code Of Conduct

Aūno is a place to build useful things together. We want everyone who takes part to feel welcome, whatever their background or experience.

## What We Expect

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Welcome questions, especially from people who are new.
- Accept a "no" on an idea gracefully, and offer one with a reason.

## What Is Not Okay

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Unwelcome comments about anyone's identity or appearance.
- Sharing someone's private information without permission.
- Repeatedly derailing discussions after being asked to stop.

## If Something Goes Wrong

Report it privately to the maintainers through the contact options on the Aūno organization page. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or block anyone who breaks these expectations.
